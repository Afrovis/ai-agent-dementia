Help improve a dementia care research project

This is a research project building software that watches over someone with dementia at night and gently helps them. I'm looking for healthy volunteers to record a short, guided video of themselves moving around a room, so the software can be tested on more varied footage than my own.

Your video stays private. It is encrypted in your browser before it leaves your computer. Only I (Mathias) can see it. The analysis runs on my own computer with local AI. Your video is never sent to any AI company or cloud service. Cloudflare delivers this website and checks for bots, but it only ever handles encrypted data it cannot read. You can ask for your video to be deleted at any time with the link you get after recording. No sound is recorded. This is a research project, not a medical service.

What the software sees

Here's a real example: a skeleton overlay and a state label (standing, walking, sitting, in bed, on the floor) drawn live on a recording of the room.
