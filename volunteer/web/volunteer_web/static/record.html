Before you record

Your video is encrypted in your browser before it leaves your computer. Only I (Mathias) can see it. The analysis runs on my own computer with local AI. Your video is never sent to any AI company or cloud service. Cloudflare delivers this website and checks for bots, but it only ever handles encrypted data it cannot read. You can ask for your video to be deleted at any time with the link you get after recording. No sound is recorded. This is a research project, not a medical service.

Set up your camera

  • Try to fit the whole floor in view.
  • Make sure the room is well lit.
  • Stand at the far end and check your whole body is in frame.
